## 🌈 What is GMB Legend?

**GMB Legend** is a multi-location local SEO management platform being built to turn fragmented Google Business Profile and local SEO work into one operational system.

> 🎯 **The mission:** make local SEO easier to understand, easier to operate, and easier to scale.

It brings the major pieces of local search operations into one connected experience:

<tr>
<td align="center">📍<br><b>Locations</b></td>
<td align="center">🩺<br><b>Profile Health</b></td>

<td align="center">📝<br><b>Content</b></td>
</tr>

## 🗺️ The Journey

GMB Legend is being developed as a **long-form product journey**, not a one-shot prototype.

### 🟣 01 — FOUNDATION

Build the visual language and the core application experience.

`UI` • `Navigation` • `Components` • `Dashboards` • `Data Views`

### 🔵 02 — OPERATIONS

Turn the foundation into a practical workspace for managing locations and local SEO activities.

`Locations` • `Profiles` • `Reviews` • `Posts` • `Products` • `SEO`

### 🟢 03 — INTELLIGENCE

Connect the operational layer with synchronization, automation, recommendations, reporting and intelligent assistance.

`Sync` • `Jobs` • `Insights` • `Automation` • `Reporting`

## 🤖 AI — The Assistant, Not the Foundation

One important principle:

> **AI should assist the workflow — not become the workflow.**

The product's structured data remains the foundation.

AI can sit on top of that foundation:

```text
📚 Understand
     ↓
💡 Suggest
     ↓
✍️ Draft
     ↓
👤 Human Review
     ↓
✅ Approve
     ↓
⚡ Execute
```

This approach keeps important operational decisions visible and controllable.
